About the dataset

Overview

A steady-state district heating simulation driven by weather-dependent consumer demand, designed to support reproducible research on computational sensing.

◇

Physics-Based Simulation

Generated using TESPy rather than physical testbed measurements. Network components and operating conditions can be modified.

⌁

Multivariate Time Series

Mass flow, pressure and temperature signals characterize the coupled thermal and hydraulic system.

◎

Virtual Sensing

Estimate pressure and temperature from mass flow measurements and component characteristics.

Simulation

Network and Operating Conditions

Four consumers are connected through feed and return pipes, a heat source, pump, valves and junctions. Supply and return water temperatures span approximately 50–90 °C and pressures 3–5 bar.

The published simulator allows researchers to adapt network topology and component characteristics. The original study uses weather-dependent demand over a year, evaluated through hourly steady-state solutions.
