I am a Ph.D. candidate at the [École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne (EPFL)](https://www.epfl.ch/), working with [Prof. Olga Fink](https://people.epfl.ch/olga.fink?lang=en) in the [Intelligent Maintenance and Operations Systems (IMOS) Laboratory](https://imos.epfl.ch/).

My research lies at the intersection of **signal processing, machine learning, and computational sensing**. I develop methods that exploit relational and temporal structure in data for sensing and monitoring of physical systems, with particular interests in inverse problems, graph-based learning, uncertainty quantification, and physics-informed machine learning.

During my Ph.D., I was a visiting researcher at the [University of Cambridge](https://www.cam.ac.uk/) with [Prof. Alice Cicirello](https://www.eng.cam.ac.uk/profiles/ac685), and at [Aalborg University](https://www.aau.dk/) in Denmark. Before joining EPFL, I received my M.Sc. in Communications Engineering from [National Tsing Hua University (NTHU)](https://nthu-en.site.nthu.edu.tw/) in Taiwan, where I worked with [Prof. Chong-Yung Chi](https://scholar.google.com.tw/citations?user=QzDAeT8AAAAJ&hl=en).

> 📣 I welcome research discussions and collaborations in signal processing, machine learning, and computational sensing. Feel free to get in touch via email.
