Open Data

I contribute to the development and public release of datasets to support reproducible research in computational sensing, signal processing, and machine learning. My collaborators and I aim to provide accessible benchmarks that capture the relational and temporal dependencies of engineered systems, together with documentation and tools for exploring the data and evaluating new methods.

Synthetic dataset

SDH

Synthetic District Heating Network. A simulation-based benchmark generated using TESPy, with thermal and hydraulic sensor signals and physics-derived information for evaluating virtual sensing and graph-based learning.

Experimental dataset

IMOS–SWIL

District Heating Dataset. Synchronized measurements from a physical district heating testbed at Aalborg University's Smart Water Infrastructures Laboratory, supporting virtual smart metering and multivariate time-series learning.
